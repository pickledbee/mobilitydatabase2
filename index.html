What makes this different

Facebook groups and classified sites make you guess. Here, every listing answers the questions buyers always ask, and the site helps you check the answers.

  • Seller-chosen condition grades. A, B, C, or Parts Only, each with a written meaning. The seller picks the grade. We do not check it.
  • A seller checklist on every listing. Powers on, brakes tested, battery tested, cleaned. These are the seller's own statements. We do not verify them.
  • Private messaging. Nobody sees your email address or phone number unless you both choose to share. Contact details cannot be typed into messages or listings.
  • Listing history. Look up a serial number to see if it has been listed on this site before. This is a count of listings only. It does not show ownership or condition.
  • Fit Finder. Enter a weight, a doorway width, and your vehicle. See only equipment that can work, and what the seller did not say.
  • Price Guide. A rough price range by type, age, and condition, plus real prices from this site. A starting point, not an appraisal.
  • Pickup and delivery are up to you. We link to independent transportation providers, but we do not arrange or guarantee any pickup or delivery.
  • Wanted Board. Post what you need. Sellers with a match are told, and they answer privately.

We never handle your money. The site does not take payments. Pay in person or arrange payment directly. Never pay with gift cards or wire transfers.

How it works

If you are buying

  1. Search or use the Fit Finder

    Filter by type, price, grade, or distance.

  2. Ask the seller

    Sign in with your email and password. Your details stay private.

  3. Arrange pickup yourself

    Meet in a public place and look at the item before you pay. Payment and transport are between you and the seller.

If you are selling

  1. Take photos and fill in the form

    About ten minutes. We show a rough price range to help.

  2. We look over your first listing

    A person reviews it for prohibited items before it goes live. Later listings go live right away.

  3. Answer messages and mark it sold

    Listing is free. Buyers pay you directly.

Need Pickup or Delivery?

Need help transporting mobility equipment? You may contact an independent mobility-equipment transportation provider, such as Mobility City, to ask about availability, pricing, and service options. All arrangements are made directly between you and the provider. Used Mobility Market is not affiliated with Mobility City.
