What makes this different

Facebook groups and classified sites make you guess. Here, every listing answers the questions buyers always ask, and the site helps you check the answers.

  • Four plain condition grades. A, B, C, or Parts Only, each with a written meaning. No more "works great" with no details.
  • A seller checklist on every listing. Powers on, brakes tested, battery tested, cleaned. Sellers must confirm all four.
  • Private messaging. Nobody sees your email address or phone number unless you both choose to share. Contact details cannot be typed into messages or listings.
  • Equipment Passport. Look up any serial number to see if it has been listed here before, how often, and by how many sellers.
  • Fit Finder. Enter a weight, a doorway width, and your vehicle. See only equipment that can work, and what the seller did not say.
  • Price Guide. A rough price range by type, age, and condition, plus real prices from this site. A starting point, not an appraisal.
  • Delivery across Wisconsin. An independent delivery partner picks it up and brings it to the buyer, with photos at pickup and delivery and a signature.
  • Wanted Board. Post what you need. Sellers with a match are told, and they answer privately.

We never handle your money. The site does not take payments. Pay in person or arrange payment directly. Never pay with gift cards or wire transfers.

How it works

If you are buying

  1. Search or use the Fit Finder

    Filter by type, price, grade, distance, or delivery.

  2. Ask the seller

    Sign in with your email and password. Your details stay private.

  3. Pick it up or have it delivered

    Meet in a public place, or request Wisconsin delivery and see the price first.

If you are selling

  1. Take photos and fill in the form

    About ten minutes. We show a rough price range to help.

  2. We check your first listing

    A person reviews it before it goes live. Later listings go live right away.

  3. Answer messages and mark it sold

    Your Verified Seller badge grows with each completed sale.

In Wisconsin? It can come to you.

Mobility City, an independent delivery partner, can pick up an item from the seller and deliver it to your door. Enter your ZIP code on any listing marked Wisconsin delivery to see the price before you decide.
