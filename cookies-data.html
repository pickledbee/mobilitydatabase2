This page explains how the site stores information in your browser, how we use email, and how long we keep data. See also our Privacy Policy.

1. Cookies and similar storage

The site does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. It does use your browser's built-in storage to run sign-in.

ItemWhereWhyHow long
Sign-in tokenBrowser local storage, only if you choose "Keep me signed in"Keeps you signed in on that deviceUntil you sign out or the session expires
Sign-in tokenBrowser session storage, if you do not choose that optionKeeps you signed in during that visitUntil you close the tab or browser window, or up to 1 day

These are needed for sign-in to work. You can clear them any time in your browser settings or by signing out.

Fonts. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads them, Google may receive your IP address and browser details.

2. Email

Account emails. When you sign up, sign in, reset a password, post a listing, receive a message, or when we need to tell you about your account or a safety issue, we email you. These are needed to run the service.

Marketing emails. We send newsletters, tips or offers only to people who tick a clear box agreeing to them, such as the weekly update. We keep a record of when and how you agreed. Every marketing email will say who it is from, use an honest subject line, include our mailing address, and have an unsubscribe link that works.

We will not buy or rent email lists, sell your email, or share it with anyone else for their marketing.

3. How long we keep data

  • Account: While your account is open. You may ask us to delete it (see below).
  • Sign-in sessions: Until they expire. Sessions end automatically, after about 1 day if you did not choose "Keep me signed in."
  • Unconfirmed sign-ups: Deleted automatically if you do not confirm within 60 minutes.
  • Listings and photos: While listed, and for a short period afterward.
  • Reports, takedown notices and records we need for safety, fraud prevention or legal reasons: As long as needed for those purposes.

Your requests. You can ask to see, fix or delete your data by writing to the contact form or using the contact form. We may keep some records if the law requires, or to deal with fraud, abuse or legal claims.

5. Security

Passwords are stored only as a salted, scrambled hash. The database is private. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited. The site uses HTTPS. If personal data is ever exposed, we will tell the people affected as the law requires.
